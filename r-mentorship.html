Transform R into a Geospatial Powerhouse

Join our 5-week virtual mentorship program and learn how to use R for spatial data analysis, remote sensing, cartography, and geovisualization.

Foundations of R for Geospatial Work
  • R & RStudio setup

Capstone Projects & Career
  • Final project presentations
