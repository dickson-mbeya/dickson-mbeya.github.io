About Me

Geospatial Expert & Biodiversity Informatician

I am a Malawian geospatial professional with over eight years of experience in GIS, remote sensing, cartography, and biodiversity informatics. I lead spatial assessments for national biodiversity policy, manage donor-funded conservation projects, and build geospatial capacity across government and academia. My work spans ecosystem mapping, land-cover classification, accessibility analysis, and web-based spatial information systems — with a consistent focus on translating technical outputs into policy-relevant evidence.
